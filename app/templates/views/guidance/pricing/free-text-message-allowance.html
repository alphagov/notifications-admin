Free text message allowance

Each eligible service you add will get an annual allowance of free text messages. The free allowance renews on 1 April.

The size of the allowance depends on the organisation you work for.

The free allowance is discretionary. We review it every year to make sure GOV.​UK Notify can still support those teams who need it the most.

If your service sends a very high volume of text messages in a single financial year, we may not renew its free allowance.

You only pay for text messages if you exceed your free allowance. Find out how much text messages cost.
