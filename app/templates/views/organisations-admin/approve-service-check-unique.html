Follow the instructions on this page to confirm whether ‘{{ current_service.name }}’ needs an annual allowance of free text messages.

Step 1: Does the service plan to send text messages?

{{ current_service.go_live_user.name }} estimates that their service will send N text messages per year.

If they do not expect to send any text messages, go to step 2.

If the expected volume is greater than zero, go to step 3.

Step 2: Does the service have any text message templates?

Check if the service has any text message templates (opens in new tab).

If it does, go to step 3.

If there are no text message templates:

  1. Select Yes or not applicable.
  2. Select Continue.

Step 3: Confirm that the service is eligible for a free allowance

Check the list of {{ current_service.organisation.name }} services (opens in new tab).

Look out for similar:

  • service names
  • message templates
  • team members

To qualify for the free allowance, ‘{{ current_service.name }}’ must be the only service of its kind run by {{ current_service.organisation.name }}.

Different campaigns or projects run by the same team or department do not qualify for a free allowance.
